We operate a strict Challenge 25 policy. Any person who appears to be under 25 years of age will be asked to provide valid photographic proof of age before being served alcohol. Acceptable forms of ID include a valid passport, a UK driving licence or a PASS-accredited proof of age card.

We will refuse service of alcohol to anyone who is under 18 years of age, or who is unable to provide satisfactory proof of age when asked. This applies to all customers and to all purchases of alcohol on the premises.
